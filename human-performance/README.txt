# 🏃 Cardiorespiratory Endurance Assessment

**Adrien Jefferson | Exercise Science Coursework**

## Overview

A course exercise using the 1.5-mile run–walk test to estimate
cardiorespiratory endurance, followed by reflections on training
experience and strategies for maintaining or improving performance.

## Recorded Results

| Measure | Recorded result |
|---|---|
| 1.5-mile completion time | 9 minutes, 23 seconds |
| Worksheet-calculated VO₂max estimate | 54.99 mL/kg/min |

The VO₂max value is a field-test estimate calculated from running
time, rather than a direct measurement of oxygen consumption.

## Topics Practiced

- Recording exercise performance.
- Converting elapsed time into decimal minutes.
- Applying a prediction equation.
- Reflecting on training habits and endurance.
